Get the Flex app

Movies, series and live TV on your phone, TV box or laptop.

Android phone or TV box

Download the app

Tap the button. When Chrome asks, tap Download, then open the file and tap Install.

Download for Android

The first time, Android may ask you to allow installing apps from Chrome. Turn it on, then go back and tap Install. Chrome may also say the file can be harmful because it isn't from Google Play. It's safe to download.

iPhone or iPad

Add Flex to your Home Screen

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (the square with an arrow).
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.
Laptop or computer

Install Flex

Click the button to add Flex to your computer. It opens in its own window, like an app.

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or open the menu (⋮) and choose Install Flex.
